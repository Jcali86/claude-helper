Beginner-Friendly Claude Help

Start here if Claude feels new.

Claude Helper is for people who want calm, clear help with Claude on a Mac. You do not need GitHub, Terminal, or Claude Code to start.

Start here I already have Claude

Do not want to install anything yet? Use Claude in your browser — no download needed.

Your first win can be this simple

Open Claude, stay in Chat, ask one honest question, and let the rest come later.

What to do right now

  1. Get Claude on your Mac or open Claude in your browser.
  2. Stay in Chat and send one simple message.
  3. Learn what Chat, Cowork, and Code mean when you are ready.
  4. Only later, if you need it, explore folders, projects, or Terminal.

You can safely ignore all of this for now

GitHub, Terminal, Claude Code, skills, connectors, and plugins are all things you do not need on day one. You do not need to understand repos, commands, or extra tools to get your first useful result.
