Step 1

Get Claude without the confusing bits.

This page is for the very start. If this feels new, that is normal. You do not need GitHub, Terminal, or Claude Code for this step.

Get Claude Desktop Use Claude in your browser

Do this in order

  1. Pick whichever feels easier right now.
    Desktop is a Claude app you install on your Mac. Browser means you just open a website — nothing to install.
  2. Open Claude.
    If you picked Desktop, follow the official Claude Desktop page and open the app after it installs. If you picked browser, open claude.ai.
  3. Sign in or create your Claude account.
  4. Stay in Chat.
    You do not need Cowork or Code yet.
  5. Copy this first message and send it:
    Hi Claude. I am new to this. Please help me get comfortable using Claude on my Mac. Keep it simple, practical, and one step at a time.

What success looks like

You are signed in, you can see Chat, and Claude replies to your first message. That is it. Everything else can wait.

If this already feels confusing

Use Claude in your browser first. That means opening a website in Safari or Chrome instead of installing anything on your Mac right away.

Once that feels easy, come back and try Claude Desktop later.

Mac words in plain English

  • Browser: the app you use to open websites, like Safari or Chrome.
  • App: a program you open on your Mac.
  • Finder: the Mac app used to look at files and folders.
  • Dock: the row of app icons usually at the bottom of your screen.
  • Spotlight: the Mac search tool. You can click the search icon on your Mac.
  • Terminal: a text window for commands. You do not need it for this step.

If you get stuck

If Claude does not open, if sign-in feels confusing, or if you are not sure what you are looking at, use the help page next.
