Your first 10 minutes with Claude

This page is designed for visual learners. It shows what beginner-friendly Claude use can look like before you are expected to understand every term.

How to use this page

  1. Read the short explanation.
  2. Look at the example screen.
  3. Copy the sample prompt and change it to fit your life.
  4. Notice that good prompts are usually simple, clear, and honest.
Minute 1 To 3

Start with Chat

`Chat` is usually the best first step because it feels closest to a normal conversation.

Try this prompt:

I am new to AI and new to using a Mac outside a browser. Please explain things simply and avoid jargon. Help me understand what Claude can do for everyday life, learning, and small projects.

Why this works: it tells Claude your level, your preference, and your goal.

I am new to AI and new to using a Mac outside a browser. Please explain things simply.
Absolutely. I will keep things practical and low-stress. Claude can help you learn, plan, write, organize, and build simple projects one step at a time.
A good place to start is: Chat for questions, Cowork for bigger guided tasks, and Code for helping inside a real project folder.
Help me plan a simple project to learn AI tools without getting overwhelmed.
Great. Let’s keep it small and useful. Week 1 can be learning how to ask better questions. Week 2 can be one simple automation or one tiny app.
I can also break each week into 15-minute tasks if that feels better.
Minute 4 To 6

Use Cowork for bigger tasks

`Cowork` is helpful when you want a more guided partner for a task with more moving parts.

Try this prompt:

Help me plan a simple project to learn AI tools without getting overwhelmed. I want small steps, plain English, and examples I could actually use at home.

Good uses include project planning, learning paths, and real-life tasks that need structure.

Minute 7 To 10

Use Code when files or Terminal are involved

`Code` is where Claude helps inside a real folder. This is useful for building tools, fixing setup issues, and explaining Terminal commands.

Try this prompt:

I am new to Terminal and coding projects. Please explain every step in plain language. Help me build a very simple app that collects positive local news and shows me a summary.

The key is telling Claude to slow down and explain things as it works.

I am new to Terminal and coding projects. Please explain every step in plain language.
Perfect. I will explain what each command does before we use it. We can begin by creating a simple folder, then a tiny web app, then test it locally.
If something could go wrong, I will pause and explain the safe next step first.

Three prompts worth keeping

Beginner mode

Please explain this as if I am smart but new to this. Use simple language and show me one step at a time.

Show me what good looks like

Give me an example of a good prompt, a good result, and why it works.

Help me stay calm

If something could break or confuse me, tell me before we do it.
